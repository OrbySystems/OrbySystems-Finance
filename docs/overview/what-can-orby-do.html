What can OrbySystems do? ​

Chat over your data ​

Ask questions in plain language and get answers grounded in your actual data — plain text, a data table, or a chart, whichever fits the question best.

Personal finance, end to end ​

  • Spending & income — every transaction auto-categorized, with recipes for spending trends, recurring bills, top merchants, and more.
  • Investment — track brokerage holdings, allocation, and performance.
  • Assets & liabilities — manually track a house, a car, or other assets, plus the loans against them, with automatic principal/interest tracking on linked payments.
  • Combined finance — bring everything together into one net worth and debt picture across all your accounts.

See the Personal Finance section for details.

Recipes and dashboards ​

Pre-built charts and breakdowns for the questions you'll ask often, with drill-downs into the details behind any number. See Browse Recipes.

Getting your data in ​

  • Statement Downloader — automatically fetch statements from supported institutions instead of downloading them by hand.
  • Statement Downloader Builder — walk through building support for a new institution yourself.
  • Transactions Extractor — parses bank and brokerage statements (PDF, CSV, spreadsheet) into clean transaction data.
  • Transactions Extractor Builder — teach OrbySystems to understand a statement format it doesn't already recognize.

Privacy tools ​

  • PII Redactor — strip personal information out of a document before sharing or saving it elsewhere.
  • PII Detector — scan a document to see what personal information it contains, without modifying it.

Working with documents ​

  • Document Chunker — break a large document into pieces ready for OrbySystems to work with.

Model tools ​

  • Model Test — check that a model you've configured works correctly with OrbySystems.
  • Model Eval Suite — measure how well and how fast a given model answers real finance questions, so you can choose with confidence.

See the Tools section for a full walkthrough of each.
