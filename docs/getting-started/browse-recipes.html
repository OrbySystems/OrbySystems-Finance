Browse Recipes ​

Recipes are pre-built charts and breakdowns over your project's data — spending by category, account balances over time, portfolio performance, debt payoff progress, and more. Opening one shows results instantly, so they're the fastest way to check something you'll want to look at often.

Opening a recipe ​

The Recipes panel sits alongside chat in your project. Pick a recipe from the list to run it and see its chart. A time-period selector (1 month up to 10 years, or a custom range) applies to whichever recipe is open, so you can quickly compare, say, this month's spending against last year's.

One recipe can be pinned as a larger summary tile, with a few other pinned recipes shown as smaller tiles beside it — a quick at-a-glance dashboard for the project.

On a Combined Finance project, a focus switcher lets you flip between views like Combined Spending, Spending, Investing, Net Worth, and Retirement, each offering its own relevant set of recipes.

Drilling into detail ​

Click a bar, category, or KPI on a chart to drill in — the tile swaps to a detailed table or breakdown for just that slice, and you can step back out to the overview. If the underlying data changes while a recipe is open (for example, a new source finishes importing), the result dims to show it's stale rather than disappearing, so you know to re-run it.

Next step ​

Recipes are great for questions you'll ask often. For anything else — or to dig deeper into a specific number — try asking questions about your data in chat.
